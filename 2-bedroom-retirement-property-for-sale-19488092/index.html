Spectacularly located within the historical heart of Winchester, this very well-presented apartment offers two bedrooms, light and spacious lounge/dining room, immaculate kitchen, large bathroom and off-road parking for one car in a secure underground carpark. No onward chain.

Lions Hall, an exclusive development for over 55's, was built in 1987 and is set within close walking distance to Winchester high street and railway station. The development offers the opportunity to live independently whilst still being able to enjoy a close community around you.

Upon entry, there is a spacious central hall that leads through to all accommodation. The light and spacious living room, with ample space for lounge and dining furniture, has an electric fireplace with mantelpiece and surround. The view is stunning, taking in Winchester Cathedral and Winchester College, as well as countryside beyond. The kitchen is fitted with a range of eye and low-level units, integrated oven, hob, space for further appliances. There are two bedrooms, one of which is a spacious double with modern sliding-door wardrobes, and the second bedroom is a versatile space that could be a guest bedroom, study or dining room. The property benefits from a lovely modern refitted bathroom, and also a utility room/WC. Further offered is a useful cloakroom for storage and a large built-in storage cupboard in the hall. The apartment has been redecorated and modernised throughout and has been very well maintained and updated by the current vendors.

Outside, the communal courtyard garden is pretty and well-maintained, and can be accessed via stairwell or lift. There is a secure allocated parking space in a below ground floor garage reached via lift and stair access, and there is also a private lock-up store room. Lions Hall further offers a social communal lounge, a laundry room, and there is also a guest suite offered at a very reasonable rate for visitors. There is a part-time warden on site, and a 24-hour facility in each apartment to call for help in an emergency.
